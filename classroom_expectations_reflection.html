Instructions

This activity is not only about saying sorry. It is about understanding what went wrong, why expectations matter, and what better choices you can make next lesson.

Use complete sentences. Try to be honest and specific. Short answers such as “I don’t know”, “nothing”, or “yes” are not enough.
